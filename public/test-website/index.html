The Mellowluv story

A small handmade jewellery brand.

A small handmade jewellery brand built on creativity, custom ideas and pieces that feel personal.

Every piece begins as an idea — a colour, a memory, a tiny detail noticed on a walk. Jars are packed by hand, custom requests are sketched over DMs, and nothing leaves the studio until it feels right.

Because the pieces are made in small batches, no two are exactly alike. Slight variations are part of the charm, not a flaw.

— Nishtha
