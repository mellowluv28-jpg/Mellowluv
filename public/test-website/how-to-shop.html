How to Shop

Six calm steps from first look to a little parcel at your door.

1

Browse and Select

Explore the collection or a mystery jar. Open any piece to see its photos, price, description and availability.

2

Add to Cart

Tap the small plus on a product card, or choose a quantity on the product page. Your bag saves in this browser.

3

Checkout

Add your name, phone, Instagram handle and full delivery address. Check every detail before continuing.

4

Pay via UPI

The live store shows a UPI QR and ID after you place an order. This preview deliberately hides both and cannot take payment.

5

Upload Screenshot

On the live store you upload a payment screenshot for manual verification. In this preview the image is only shown locally and never uploaded.

6

Order Confirmation

Once payment is verified, the studio confirms the order, makes your piece and starts the tracking timeline.

Ready to try the flow?

Everything is safe to click. No real order, payment or message is created.
