All orders must be paid in advance via UPI. There is no cash on delivery. Your order is confirmed only after we verify your payment; until then it stays in an unpaid state.

Please include your order number in the payment note. Paying a different UPI ID, or sending an incomplete amount, will delay verification.

By placing an order you agree to all policies on this page. If anything is unclear, message @mellowluvv_ before ordering.

Handmade jewellery takes time and care. Please allow 10–15 days for making and dispatch. Normal delivery adds roughly 4–6 days of shipping depending on your location.

Need it sooner? Select Urgent at checkout for ₹50 extra. Urgent orders are prioritised, made in about 2–3 days and dispatched sooner.

Every piece is handmade with love and takes its own time. Please be patient with dispatch — and choose urgent making if you need it early.

Shipping is charged separately at checkout. Jewellery shipping is ₹70 by default; the studio will contact you on Instagram if a large order needs a higher charge.

Parcels are sent with tracking. Once the package is handed to the courier, delivery delays, lost packages and transit damage are the courier's responsibility. We will help where we can, but the courier handles delivery issues after dispatch.

If a parcel arrives damaged, keep a clear unboxing video. It is the only proof accepted for a courier claim.

We do not accept cancellations, refunds or returns unless the mistake is from our side — for example a wrong product, wrong size shipped or a defective item. Please choose carefully before ordering.

If something is wrong, message us within 48 hours of delivery with your unboxing video. We will replace the item or issue a refund where the mistake is ours.

Change-of-mind returns, damage caused after delivery and requests without an unboxing video cannot be accepted.

Custom pieces are always discussed first. DM @mellowluvv_ with your idea, colours and references so pricing and timing can be confirmed.

Do not place a fully custom order through the regular checkout before talking to us — custom work depends on materials, complexity and current workload.

Mystery jars and mix jars accept preferences. Mention the theme you want before confirming, otherwise the jar is packed as a surprise.

Keep your pieces away from water, perfume, lotions and harsh cleaners. Put jewellery on last when getting ready, and take it off before swimming or bathing.

Store each piece dry and separately to avoid scratches. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after wearing.

Handmade pieces have small natural variations in colour, shape and finish. These are part of the character of the piece, not a defect.
